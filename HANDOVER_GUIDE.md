# AI Precision Beauty Intelligence Suite - Handover Guide

## 1. Executive Summary
The Sales-Gated Analysis Quota system has been fully expanded into a comprehensive AI Intelligence Suite. The platform now supports advanced team management, ROI-driven analytics, and an enhanced customer engagement engine through social sharing and AI-driven concierge services.

## 2. Key Features Implemented

### A. Team & Quota Management (Clinic Owner)
- **Sales-Gated Quota Model**: Quotas are now strictly tied to individual sales users rather than the center, allowing for granular tracking of performance and resource usage.
- **Bulk Quota Allocation**: Efficient interface for owners to distribute quota to the entire team or selected members in a single action.
- **AI Quota Rebalancing**: A proactive recommendation engine that identifies high-yield performers running low on quota and suggests reallocating surplus from lower-yield nodes.
- **Quota Transfer History**: Complete audit log of all resource movements between nodes.

### B. Aesthetic ROI Analytics (Executive Dashboard)
- **Conversion Funnel**: Visual tracking from AI Scan -> Engagement -> Lead Conversion -> Booking.
- **AI-Driven Revenue Tracking**: Quantifies the financial impact of AI interactions on clinic inflow.
- **Sales Yield Matrix**: A sophisticated scatter-plot analysis mapping individual scan volume against generated revenue and conversion rates.
- **Period-over-Period (PoP) Growth**: Dynamic growth indicators for all key metrics across 7d, 30d, and 90d cycles.

### C. Customer Engagement Engine
- **Verified Achievement Sharing**: Beautiful, social-media-ready cards for "Aesthetic Milestones" and "Analysis Results" with unique public verified links.
- **Before & After Comparison**: Publicly shareable progress reports that quantify improvement over time across multiple biological nodes (spots, pores, wrinkles).
- **Engagement Telemetry**: Advanced tracking of public report interaction (active duration, scroll depth, interactions) that automatically boosts Lead Scores for high-engagement prospects.
- **Roadmap-Aware AI Concierge**: An intelligent virtual assistant that provides personalized advice by pulling data from the customer's active Treatment Roadmap and latest scan results.
